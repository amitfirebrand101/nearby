A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Little answers.

What’s the difference between a Pingg and a Live Like?+

A Pingg says you’d like to break the ice and gives them a choice to respond. A Live Like is appreciation without that invitation: a little feel-good moment, with no conversation expected.

Is Pingg only for dating?+

No. Pingg is for everyday social moments—friends, creative collaborators, gig buddies, neighbours or someone you’d like to say hello to. It is an invitation, not an assumption.

Does the other person need the app?+

Yes. Discovery is between people using Nearby who have chosen to be visible. You can’t Pingg just any phone around you.

Can I download it right now?+

We’re running controlled Android pilot tests, not a public App Store or Play Store launch. Join the list for testing invitations and availability updates. iPhone support isn’t part of the current pilot.

Are these the current app screens?+

These are concept designs for the direction we’re exploring. The current Android pilot looks different. Concept-only elements—such as distance labels, extra social channels and verified-profile controls—aren’t promises of available features.

Will someone see my socials automatically?+

No. Your name and photo are visible while you choose to be discoverable. Sharing contact details is a separate, per-person choice. You can ignore or hold a Pingg without sharing anything.
