The simple version

A better first move.

The app is designed for the tiny window between noticing someone and wondering “what if?”

01

Turn on Nearby mode when you feel open to a hello. Turn it off whenever you want.

02

Built for real life

Less performance. More possibility.

Nearby gives a little structure to a very human moment — and leaves the decision with both people.
