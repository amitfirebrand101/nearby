A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Little answers.

Is this only for dating?+

No. The idea is appreciation and connection in real life—friends, creative collaborators, gig buddies or someone you’d like to get to know. A Ping is an invitation, not an assumption.

Does the other person need the app?+

Yes. Discovery is between people using Nearby who have chosen to be visible. You can’t Ping just any phone around you.

Can I download it right now?+

We’re running controlled Android pilot tests, not a public App Store or Play Store launch. Join the list for testing invitations and availability updates. iPhone support isn’t part of the current pilot.

Are these the current app screens?+

These are concept designs for the direction we’re exploring. The current Android pilot looks different. Concept-only elements—such as distance labels, extra social channels and verified-profile controls—aren’t promises of available features.

Will someone see my socials automatically?+

No. Your name and photo are visible while you choose to be discoverable. Sharing contact details is a separate, per-connection choice. You can ignore or hold a Ping without sharing anything.
